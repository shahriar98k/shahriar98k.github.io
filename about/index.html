My story, short

I started with social media for one client. Then he asked for SEO and I knew nothing about it. So I tested, broke things and fixed them. Three months later, 5 or 6 keywords were at the top of Google USA. That client is still with me.

Since then: online stores, clinics, hotels, staffing firms and builders. The USA, UK, Italy, Pakistan and a lot more. I also write, design, edit videos and run ads, because I can't sit still.

Before all this, I studied apparel engineering at Bangladesh University of Textiles and garments business at IBA, University of Dhaka. I also work in the apparel industry. So it's fabric by day, Google after hours.

One day I want to build a sustainable luxury fashion house. Until then, I'll keep making websites easier to find.
